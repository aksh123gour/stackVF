GOOGLE SHEETS DATA HUB

Master Telemetry & Analytics Hub

Connect your private Google Sheet as the central data engine. Every sync automatically builds and updates executive KPI tiles, PR & 1RM tracking, volume heatmaps, granular set telemetry, and embedded charts.

Generated Automatically on Every Sync:
🏆 PR & Strength Progress — All-time PRs, rep counts, and Brzycki 1RM progression.
📈 Executive Hub — Performance KPI tiles with automated Volume & Bodyweight charts.
🏋️ Workouts Log — Complete session log with 3-color Volume gradient heatmaps.
📊 Sets & Reps Detail — Granular set-by-set telemetry for custom analytics & pivot tables.
📅 Consistency Heatmap — Color-coded calendar matrix for training & nutrition habits.
🥗 Nutrition & Hydration — Daily calorie, protein, macro, and water compliance logs.
⚖️ Bodyweight & Composition — Weight trendline with 7-day moving averages.
1-Minute Setup Guide:
  1. Create a new spreadsheet at sheets.new (e.g. named STACK Fitness Hub).
  2. In Google Sheets, click Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Delete any existing code in Code.gs.
  4. Click Copy Apps Script Code below and paste it into Google Apps Script. Click Save.
  5. Click Deploy → New deployment → choose Web app.
  6. Set Execute as: Me, and Who has access: Anyone.
  7. Click Deploy, approve Google permissions, and copy the Web app URL (ends in /exec).
  8. Paste the URL into STACK Settings and tap Sync Now!
